\section{Agent framework and score definition}
The six primary voting agents are fundamental, risk, forecast, comparison, quality, and liquidity. Four further integration/governance components must not be counted as independent investment-vote agents. The investment score takes confidence-weighted votes \emph{after} market-relative risk and sector-peer adjustments. The vote inclusion threshold is 0.30:
\begin{equation}
S_i =
\frac{\sum_{a\in A_i} c_{ia} v_{ia}^{*}}
{\sum_{a\in A_i} c_{ia}}, \qquad
A_i = \{a:c_{ia}\geq0.30\},\qquad v_{ia}^{*}\in[-1,1].
\label{eq:weighted}
\end{equation}
Raw vote means below are \emph{not} the weighted inputs in Equation~\eqref{eq:weighted}; risk/peer votes can be transformed in investment mode. Evidence constraints, data availability, disagreements and governance downgrades further change the final tier.

\section{Germany: identified feed failure and controlled recovery test}
The saved German stage-1 status for 20 October records the root cause: Deutsche B\"orse's daily delayed post-trade object was not downloadable at scan time; the software selected its fallback Twelve Data batch feed, which then failed because \texttt{BIAP\_GLOBAL\_MARKET\_API\_KEY} was absent. As a result, both German venues reported zero market coverage and \texttt{rankingEligible=false} despite pre-existing company financial results.

\section{Pre-registered forward-test formulae (not yet evaluated)}
All outcomes here remain \emph{pending} until the chosen 20 November 2026 horizon. Adjust both opening and closing prices consistently for corporate actions, and report missing exits.
\begin{equation}
R_{i,H}=\frac{P_{i,H}^{adj}}{P_{i,0}^{adj}}-1,\qquad
R_{m,g,H}=\frac{1}{|E_{m,g,H}|}\sum_{i\in E_{m,g,H}}R_{i,H}.
\label{eq:returns}
\end{equation}
The sell basket is an \emph{underperformance comparator}, not an assumed implemented short book:
\begin{equation}
\Delta_{m,H}=R_{m,\mathrm{BUY},H}-R_{m,\mathrm{SELL},H},\qquad
\alpha_{m,H}=R_{m,\mathrm{BUY},H}-R_{m,\mathrm{benchmark},H}.
\label{eq:spread}
\end{equation}
Before observing future returns, register cross-venue aggregation weights; stratify bootstrap replicates by venue and report confidence intervals, missingness, and calendar/FX treatment. For same-date market activity exploration, pre-specify:
\begin{equation}
\rho_{S,V}=
\operatorname{Corr}\!\bigl(\operatorname{rank}(S_i),
                           \operatorname{rank}(\operatorname{tradingValue}_i)\bigr).
\label{eq:spearman}
\end{equation}
For genuine agent contribution evidence, run prospective leave-one-agent-out \emph{rerankings} and compare outcomes, rather than inferring causality from conditional raw vote averages. No outcome-level $p$-values, Sharpe ratios or predictive ROC-AUC estimates are claimed.

\section{Limitations and conclusion}
A true pre-freeze point-in-time backtest is not possible with the archived versioned fundamentals, membership history and past decision rules. Other limitations are selection bias, US cohort dominance, peer scarcity, nontrading securities, different price units/currencies, corporate actions, and German market-feed downtime.

\textbf{Conclusion.} The documented evidence establishes an auditable ten-country forward-test baseline, six recoverable primary agent signals, 964 frozen BUY/SELL decisions and 97.61\% date-aligned entry-price coverage. The study has \emph{not} yet established investment profitability, benchmark outperformance or causal agent importance.
