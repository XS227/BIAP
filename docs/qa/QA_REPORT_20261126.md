Findings:
- **Missing prices** are a coverage matter, not a fault: stored prices exist for the markets the background jobs refresh (US, DE, GB, FR, IT, SE, NO, DK, FI, NL, ES, ...). Markets outside the background allowlist (IN, JP, KR, SA, HK, SG, ZA, CA TSXV, NZ) have almost no stored price or statements; they are priced live on demand only.
- **Stale prices**: 41 stocks have a last bar more than 7 days older than the newest bar (10 more than 30 days): IN BSE 25, AE ADX 9, US 3, others 1-2. No stored price is zero or negative.
- **Missing statements**: 19,269 stocks (63%) have no stored fundamentals file; another 1,690 have partial statements. Per market in section 4.
- **Stale filings**: 179 stocks have a latest filing older than 18 months (GB 16, DE 36, CA 7, US 26, AU 21, ...); 731 have no filing date.
- **Impossible values**: 637 stocks with at least one flag (HK 204, US 210, AU 119, ...). The hard defects that made the Risk Agent give a wrong level (assets <= 0, equity above assets, liabilities above 50x assets, negative liabilities) affected 40 stocks, 38 of which had a level: fixed (B5).
- **Currency / units**: 1,004 stocks report in a currency different from the listing currency (ADRs, dual listings; GB 149, HK 281, CA 125, US 195): the engine converts with the statement-date FX rate, so this is expected; no unit error was found among stocks that have statements (unit scale SUSPECT only 9, DE Frankfurt 7, GB 1, DE Xetra 1; UNKNOWN is shown for stocks without statements).
- **Germany**: statements exist (only 8-10 missing) but only 6-7% give at least 2 solvency models: the German source lacks fields (current assets/liabilities, retained earnings). Needs a source decision (remaining R3).

## 8. Turning the agents on in production: exact steps and rollback (NOT done, waiting for your approval)
Flags: `DECISION_ACCURACY_AGENT_ENABLED`, `RISK_AGENT_ENABLED`, `PERFORMANCE_AGENT_ENABLED` in `/home/ubuntu/biap-global/BIAP/analysis/.env.global` (read by `biap-global.service` through `EnvironmentFile`). Restart rule available to the `ubuntu` user: `sudo systemctl restart biap-global.service`. Only one flag per day; each step separately approved.
**Before any flag**
1. Merge #121 to #124 (and #120 once the calibration decision is made), then deploy main to the production checkout (`/home/ubuntu/biap-global/BIAP`) with the usual release step; the flags are still off, so behaviour for users is unchanged. Confirm `/global/status` shows all three agent flags `false`.
2. Run `python -m global_markets.filing_backfill --market <CC> <EXCHANGE> --dry-run` for each market you want covered, then without `--dry-run` with `--max 200 --delay 5` outside 22:45-02:00 UTC (and not during the 17:00-19:10 EU and 21:15-22:15 US refresh windows). Repeat until `toFetch` is 0.
3. Apply the approved Risk calibration (config + tests) if you approve proposal D.
4. Back up the env file: `cp .env.global .env.global.bak-$(date -u +%Y%m%d)`.
**Step A: Decision Accuracy (no per-request cost)**
- Add `DECISION_ACCURACY_AGENT_ENABLED=1`; `sudo systemctl restart biap-global.service`; wait for the service to answer `/health`.
- Check: `/global/status` has `decisionAccuracyEnabled: true`; `/global/accuracy/summary?horizon=1` returns venue-equal BUY -0.35%, SELL -10.93%, spread 10.57 pp; the app shows the Track record card and More item; horizons 3/6/12 show "pending".
**Step B (24 h later): Risk**
- Add `RISK_AGENT_ENABLED=1`; restart; check `/global/status` `riskAgentEnabled: true`; `POST /global/risk` for 5 stocks (US, DE, IT, JP, one unsupported) each answers in under 1 s with no outbound connection (journal shows no download), JP 5243 gives 422, IR gives 404; the stock page shows the Risk section.
**Step C (24 h later): Performance** — same pattern with `PERFORMANCE_AGENT_ENABLED=1`.
**Watch after each step (30 minutes, then next morning):** `journalctl -u biap-global --since "-30min"` shows no tracebacks and no 5xx; latency of the new routes under 1 s; memory of the service under 500 MB.
**Rollback (under one minute, no data migration; agents are read-only)**
- Remove the added line (or set it to `0`) in `.env.global`, then `sudo systemctl restart biap-global.service`. The app hides the section automatically (it reads the flags from `/global/status`; a disabled agent answers 404).
- If a code release must be undone: revert the merge commit(s) on main, redeploy, restart. Restore the env backup with `cp .env.global.bak-<date> .env.global` if a flag edit went wrong.
- Do not restart during the 22:45-02:00 UTC window or while a price refresh is running.
