# Risk Agent levels: D2 (owner-approved 2026-11-27), preliminary calibration

Config `analysis/global_markets/data/risk_agent_config.json` version 5 (`rules`), logic in `risk_agent.assess` (response `version` 2).

## Rules
Severe signals counted (4): Altman Z'' in the distress zone (Z'' variant, 1995, cut-off **1.1**; the 1.81 cut-off belongs to the 1968 original, which is not used), Ohlson >= 0.50, Zmijewski >= 0.5, interest coverage < 1.0. Elevated signs: the same four at their elevated limits (Ohlson >= 0.038, Zmijewski >= 0.35, Altman grey zone, coverage < 1.5), price volatility/drawdown >= 50%, and negative equity (counts as one sign). Beneish is shown as a reason only.

| Level | Rule |
|---|---|
| CRITICAL | going-concern text, or all 4 severe and negative equity |
| HIGH | 3 of the 4 severe |
| MEDIUM | at least 1 severe, or 2 or more elevated signs |
| LOW | everything else; a single elevated sign adds a `watch` note |
| no level | fewer than 2 solvency models and no flag at all; impossible statements (#124); financial institutions with no flag |

New response fields: `watch` (list), `calibration` (`preliminary` for US, `not_calibrated` for every other market, `unavailable` when there is no level). A level given on fewer than 2 solvency models has `status: PARTIAL` and the reason "statements are partial ... LOW is not a clean result". Config rules are validated (positive integers, critical >= high >= medium).
